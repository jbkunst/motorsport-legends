# motorsport-legends

A personal database of race cars, homologation specials, performance icons, design legends, and 1:64 scale models connected to automotive culture.

The project started from motorsport history and racing results, especially endurance events such as Le Mans, Daytona, Nürburgring and Sebring. Over time, it also includes historically relevant road cars, design icons, and collectible 1:64 models whose importance comes from engineering, culture, design, or their relationship with performance and racing heritage.
